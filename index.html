Privacy Policy

GMChess: Chess Analysis & Puzzles (Android app) · Last updated: 25 October 2026

  • No account, no sign-up. The app does not ask for your name, email or password.
  • Your games stay on your phone. Games, analysis, puzzles, progress and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run a server and never receive them.
  • Game import sends the username you type to Chess.com or Lichess to download that player's public games.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect device information to show and measure ads. In the EU/UK you are asked for consent first.

1. Who we are

This policy explains how the Android app GMChess: Chess Analysis & Puzzles ("the app", "we") handles information. If you have any question about it, contact us at YOUR_CONTACT_EMAIL.

2. Information stored on your device

The app works offline and keeps its data in a local database on your phone:

  • chess games you import or paste (PGN), and the usernames you import them for
  • engine analysis of those games, run on your phone by the built-in Stockfish engine
  • puzzles made from your mistakes, the bundled puzzle collection and your progress on them
  • your daily streak, settings and preferences

This data is not sent to us or anyone else. You can delete your games and reset puzzle progress in the app's Settings ("Delete all games", "Reset puzzles"). All of it is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

3. Game import from Chess.com and Lichess

When you import games, the app sends the username you entered to the public API of the site you chose:

  • Chess.com (api.chess.com) – Chess.com privacy policy
  • Lichess (lichess.org) – Lichess privacy policy

These requests download publicly available games. As with any internet request, those services receive your device's IP address. The app does not log in to these sites and does not access any private account data.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on some screens, occasional full-screen ads, and an optional rewarded ad you can choose to watch to unlock focus mode for the day. To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version)
  • interactions with ads (views and taps)

This information is collected by Google, not by us; we only see aggregated reports (for example, how many ads were shown). Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices:

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent message. You can refuse, and you can change your choice later.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

5. Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or payment information. The app has no analytics or crash-reporting service of its own and no user accounts.

6. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it except as described above: the username you enter with Chess.com or Lichess for importing, and device information collected by Google AdMob for ads.

7. Security

Requests to Chess.com, Lichess and Google are made over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Data stored on your phone is protected by Android's app sandbox, so other apps cannot read it.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Third-party content and credits

The bundled puzzles come from the Lichess open puzzle database (CC0). Analysis uses the open-source Stockfish engine (GPLv3), running on your device.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the app gains new features. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
